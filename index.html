2 · Print settings

Orientation
Stripe direction
Picture fit
A third of the pitch (33%) is the standard. Narrow slits are sharper but darker, wide slits are brighter but blurrier. The stripe film depends only on the pitch and slit, so one film works with any number of frames.

Printing tips

  1. Print both files at 100%. Turn off “fit to page”. Measure the scale bar with a ruler.
  2. Print the picture on paper, or on backlit film for a light box.
  3. Print the stripe file on transparency film in black only, or have it output on imagesetter film (菲林).
  4. Lay the film on the picture, stripe side down. Slide it by one pitch, parallel to the stripes.
